Day 1 of learning Azure DevOps in public.

Imagine your app is a restaurant.

**IaaS** — rent the whole kitchen (Azure VMs). You own the mess.  
**PaaS** — shared kitchen with a manager (App Service, Functions, Azure SQL). You own the recipe.  
**SaaS** — takeout (Microsoft 365, GitHub). You configure; you don’t build the kitchen.

**Region** = city. **Availability Zone** = different building in that city.

One-liner: Region = city · Zone = building · IaaS/PaaS/SaaS = who holds the spatula.
