# YouTube Shorts / Community — Day 1

## Shorts script (60–90 seconds)

Talk to camera or screen-record a simple diagram. No music required.

**[0:00–0:08]**  
“Day 1 of learning Azure DevOps in public. Quick model that actually sticks.”

**[0:08–0:25]**  
“Think of your app as a restaurant.  
IaaS — you rent the whole kitchen. Azure VMs. You patch, you own the fire.  
PaaS — shared kitchen with a manager. App Service, Functions. You own the recipe.  
SaaS — takeout. GitHub, M365. You just order.”

**[0:25–0:50]**  
“Two map words people mix up:  
Region is the city.  
Availability Zone is a different building in that city — own power, own network.  
Building floods? Other building can still serve lunch.”

**[0:50–0:75]**  
“One-liner: Region equals city. Zone equals building. IaaS PaaS SaaS equals who holds the spatula.  
Lab notes are on my GitHub — link in description.”

**[0:75–0:90]**  
“Tomorrow: Portal vs CLI vs PowerShell. See you there.”
