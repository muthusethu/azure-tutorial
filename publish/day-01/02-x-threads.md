**1/**
Day 1 of learning Azure DevOps in public.

Your app is a restaurant.
Who holds the spatula when dinner goes wrong?

**2/**
IaaS = rent the whole kitchen.
You patch the OS, own the stove, clean the grease trap.
Azure example: Virtual Machines.
Max control. Max “why is this on fire.”

**3/**
PaaS = shared kitchen with a manager.
You cook. They fix the oven.
Azure examples: App Service, Functions, Azure SQL.
You own the recipe — not the building.

**4/**
SaaS = takeout.
You tap “biryani” and complain about delivery.
Examples: Microsoft 365, GitHub.
You configure. You don’t build the kitchen.

**5/**
Region = which city your restaurant is in.
Central India ≠ East US.
Latency, laws, and “why is this slow” live here.

**6/**
Availability Zone = a different building in the same city.
Own power. Own network.
Building A floods → Building B still serves lunch.

**7/**
One-liner:

Region = city  
Zone = building  
IaaS/PaaS/SaaS = who holds the spatula
